Reckoner

A single comparison view for demand planners weighing seven statistical forecasting models against each other, so the best fit for a SKU's seasonality is obvious without reading a formula.

ROLE
Product Designer
YEAR
2021
SCOPE
Demand forecasting, 7 models
OUTPUT
Web dashboard
TEAM
Accenture
The challenge

Supply and operations planners were comparing forecasting models by eye, flipping between spreadsheets exported from different statistical engines. There was no single view to see which model actually fit a given SKU's seasonality, so decisions defaulted to whichever model planners knew best — not necessarily the most accurate one.

The approach

I designed a single comparison view that plots TBATS, ARIMA and Holt-Winters forecasts against historical demand side by side, with accuracy scoring surfaced inline so the best-fitting model is obvious at a glance. Planners can tune seasonality assumptions and watch the forecast update in place.

  • TBATS
  • ARIMA
  • Holt-Winters
The outcome

Planners could compare models and pick one directly from the chart, replacing a spreadsheet-and-explanation workflow with a single screen anyone on the team could read.
