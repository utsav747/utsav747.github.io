Nest

A guided setup that walks a client transition team through the dozens of configuration steps a new engagement needs, replacing the scattered spreadsheets and trackers teams were stitching together by hand.

ROLE
Product Designer
YEAR
2023
SCOPE
Client transitions, guided setup
OUTPUT
Web dashboard
TEAM
Accenture
The challenge

Every new client transition meant re-deriving the same checklist of configuration steps — deal demographics, key attributes, ownership — usually split across a handful of spreadsheets nobody fully trusted. Nothing showed a PMO lead which steps were actually done versus just started.

The approach

I structured the transition into a single guided flow: each configuration step is its own stage with clear inputs and a visible status, so a PMO lead can see deal demographics and key attributes settle into place stage by stage instead of hunting across trackers.

  • Guided setup
  • Status tracking
  • PMO handoff
The outcome

Transition teams worked through one guided flow instead of scattered trackers, giving PMO leads a single place to see which configuration steps were actually complete.
