Process Pulse

A Sankey-based dashboard that traces every invoice through sourcing, indexing, matching, exceptions and posting — surfacing straight-through rates and exception hotspots at a glance.

ROLE
Product Designer
YEAR
2022
SCOPE
Procure-to-pay, 5 stages
OUTPUT
Web dashboard
TEAM
Accenture
The challenge

The procure-to-pay process runs through five stages, and understanding where invoices flowed straight through versus stalled meant a spreadsheet and a lot of explaining.

The approach

I designed a Sankey-based flow that traces every invoice from source to posting, with straight-through rates and exception hotspots surfaced directly on the flow so the state of the whole pipeline reads at a glance.

  • Sankey flow
  • Data viz
  • Dashboard
The outcome

A five-stage pipeline became a single, readable flow — no walkthrough needed to see where invoices move cleanly and where exceptions pile up.
