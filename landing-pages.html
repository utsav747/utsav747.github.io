Landing Pages

Pitch narratives turned into polished single-page stories the team could walk a client through live, or send on as a follow-up link instead of a slide deck.

ROLE
Product Designer
YEAR
2024
SCOPE
Client pitches, single-page
OUTPUT
Marketing pages
TEAM
Accenture
The challenge

Client pitches were being assembled as slide decks, which meant a lot of manual reformatting every time a narrative changed, and no easy way to share a live link before a call.

The approach

I turned the recurring pitch structure into a set of landing page templates — a narrative flow the team could drop content into for each client, mixing full-bleed imagery with data pull-quotes so presenters could walk through it live or send it as a follow-up.

  • Narrative
  • Layout system
  • Client handoff
The outcome

The team could reuse the same structure across pitches instead of rebuilding decks from scratch, walking clients through a single page instead of a slide-by-slide narration.
